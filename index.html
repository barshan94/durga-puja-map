MILESTONE 01 · PHOTO GPS

Turn your photos into a festival map.

Start with the photos you already took. Extract embedded GPS and capture times, inspect points on a map, and export a tidy inventory. This is the foundation for counting distinct Puja venues and planning next year's route.

🔒
Your photos stay on your device.
Files are read in this browser tab only. They are not uploaded by this app. If a photo exists only in Google Photos cloud storage, download it to your device first or select it through the photo picker if available.

01 / Import photos

Choose a manageable batch first (try 20–50 photos). For Google Photos, select the photos and their matching .json sidecars together. JPEG and HEIC/HEIF metadata support depends on the browser and file.
